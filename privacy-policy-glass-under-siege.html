Glass Under Siege — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 26 October 2026

Publisher and contact
Glass Under Siege is published by Noble Services Scotland Ltd. For privacy questions, contact enquiries@nobleservicesscotland.com.

Personal data
The app does not collect personal data. It has no player accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics or automatic diagnostic uploads. We do not receive your identity, payment details, gameplay activity or device identifiers from the app.

Local game information
Game progress, scores and settings are stored locally on your device. Verified Pro purchase information is also stored locally using protected platform storage so that previously verified Pro access can work offline. This includes purchase identifiers and verification evidence, not your name or payment details. This information is not sent to us. Protected purchase information may remain after deleting the app, depending on the operating system. The operating system may include local game information in backups according to your device settings.

Purchases and restoration
On iPhone and iPad, purchases and restoration are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes the transaction under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. The app receives the product, transaction and ownership information needed to verify Pro access, restore it and reconcile refunds or revocations. It does not receive your card details or Apple Account password. On Android, the corresponding purchase processing is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not operate a purchasing backend or send purchase records to Unity analytics.

Offline use
Gameplay and this complete policy are available offline. Previously verified Pro access is retained when the store cannot be reached. Buying, restoring and checking current store ownership can require a store connection. An offline device may retain access until a later store check establishes a refund or revocation.

Support and this policy online
If you choose to email us outside the app, we receive the address and information you include so that we can respond. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. We use support correspondence to handle your enquiry and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Visiting the separately hosted policy page is optional; the host may process ordinary web request information under its own arrangements. The app does not need to visit that website to display this policy.

Children and changes
The app does not collect personal data from children or other players. Any future change to the app's data practices will require an updated policy and the applicable store disclosures. Contact Noble Services Scotland Ltd at enquiries@nobleservicesscotland.com with questions about this policy.
